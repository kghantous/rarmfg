About the shop

RAR Manufacturing, LLC is a small shop, in business for over thirty years, specializing in design and manufacture with a large base of engineering and manufacturing expertise to draw from. Our services include small-lot production, prototyping, engineering, reverse engineering, made-to-order and engineered-to-order parts. We also have secondary operations facilities for plating, anodizing, heat treating, and completing larger fabrication jobs. Materials we work in include carbon alloys, tool steels, aluminum, brass, stainless steels, titanium, and plastics. We have extensive experience designing in both 2-D and 3-D CAD. Our expertise has been utilized in such industries as aerospace, automotive, agriculture, non-destructive testing, packaging machinery, building, inspection, automation, robotics, special machinery, and machine tools.

We specialize in giving our customers a one-on-one personalized relationship. Our goal is to provide assistance and solutions for individuals who would like to develop an idea or have a special need for a product or part. We strive to work with clients from concept to complete product — including the design, manufacture, and controls (pneumatic, hydraulic, and electrical).

Richard A. Roth, owner, has been a mechanical engineer for over forty years. He graduated from the University of Cincinnati with a Bachelor's Degree in Mechanical Engineering and worked in the machine tool and OEM packaging machinery industries for many years. In 1992, Richard started a small business with the goal of solving industrial problems by engineering and then building solutions. After acquiring several machine tools to aid with manufacturing projects, Richard built a 2,600 square-foot shop in 1996. RAR Manufacturing has been designing and engineering solutions from that shop ever since.

What we do

Six ways of working.

From a sketch on a napkin to a finished part — six things we do every week.

Small-lot production

Runs of one to a few hundred, machined to print, with single-operator accountability.

Prototyping

Iterating on parts that have to function — not just look the part.

Engineering

Mechanical design grounded in over forty years of experience. 2-D and 3-D CAD across formats.

Reverse engineering

Working from an existing part to recreate drawings, replacements, or improvements.

Made-to-order

One-off parts and assemblies when the off-the-shelf answer doesn't exist.

Engineered-to-order

Concept-through-completion engineering for custom equipment, fixtures, and tooling — including the controls.
