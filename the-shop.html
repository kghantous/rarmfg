The Shop

A 2,600 square-foot shop in the Greater Cincinnati area, built in 1996 and in continuous operation since. What follows is a walk through what's on the floor — organized as it was on the original site, by class of machine.

Shop overview

The shop is one open room — modest, well-organized, lit from the windows and overhead. An I-beam gantry crane runs across the center for moving heavy stock and finished assemblies.

Milling machines

Vertical knee-mills handle the bulk of milling work — squaring, slotting, facing, drilling, boring, and the everyday work of bringing a piece of stock to print. Good for one-offs and small runs alike.

Lathes

Engine lathes for turning, facing, threading, and boring round work — shafts, bushings, fittings, and the like. The size of the work tends to stay in a comfortable range that fits the shop.

Grinders

Surface and tool grinders for finishing flat work and sharpening cutters. Where milling and turning leave a part, grinding takes it the rest of the way to tolerance and finish.

Saws

Horizontal and vertical bandsaws for cutting bar stock and plate to rough size — the first step on most jobs that come in.

Inspection

Calipers, micrometers, gauge blocks, height gauges, and the everyday measuring tools that confirm a finished part matches the drawing.

Welding & fabrication

MIG and TIG welding for joining machined parts into larger assemblies, and for fabricating brackets, frames, and weldments to print.

Special machines

Custom equipment and assemblies built for specific jobs — the kind of one-off machinery that doesn't have a part number in any catalog.

Wood working

A wood-working corner for pattern work, jigs, fixtures, crating, and the occasional finished piece — for the times when metal isn't what the job calls for.

Miscellaneous

Drill presses, a heat-treat setup, hand tools, tooling, and the smaller equipment that supports the larger machines.
