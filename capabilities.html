What we do

Services

From a sketch on a napkin to a finished part — six things we do every week.

Small-lot production

Runs of one to a few hundred parts, machined to print with single-operator accountability.

Prototyping

Iterating on parts that have to function in the real world — not just look the part on a screen.

Engineering

Mechanical design, grounded in over forty years of professional experience across the machine-tool and OEM packaging-machinery industries.

Reverse engineering

Working from an existing part — measured, modeled, and re-drawn — to produce replacements, drawings, or improvements.

Made-to-order

One-off parts and assemblies, for the times when the off-the-shelf answer doesn't exist.

Engineered-to-order

Concept-through-completion engineering for custom equipment, fixtures, and tooling — including the controls (see below).

Materials

Common materials in the shop, in roughly the order they show up on jobs:

  • Carbon alloys
  • Tool steels
  • Aluminum
  • Brass
  • Stainless steels
  • Titanium
  • Plastics

Secondary operations

Beyond what's machined in-house, we coordinate plating, anodizing, and heat treating through trusted local sources, and we handle larger fabrication jobs that need more space or capacity than the shop carries on its own. The job is delivered as one finished part — not a stack of operations for the customer to manage.

Controls

Custom equipment usually needs a way to be run. We design and build the controls in-house: pneumatic, hydraulic, and electrical. A finished machine arrives wired, plumbed, and ready to be plugged in — not a mechanical assembly waiting for someone else's integration work.
